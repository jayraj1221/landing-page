KALAPRITI
Interactive Exhibition Landing Page — Designer Brief
“Loops of love, from hands to heart.”
A QR/scan-based mini brand experience for the Kalapriti college exhibition stall.
The page should feel warm, handmade, playful, premium and memorable.
1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
Kalapriti will have a stall at a college exhibition. Visitors will scan a QR code at the stall and land on a mobile-first Kalapriti landing page that introduces the brand through an interactive, story-driven experience.
Core idea
The landing page should feel like entering a tiny handmade world where yarn balls, crochet accessories and craft elements are alive. They welcome the visitor, introduce Kalapriti, show the brand story and products, and finally invite the visitor to connect on Instagram or contact the founder.
Main user journey
Scan QR code at the exhibition stall.
Welcome animation: cute yarn balls/crochet accessories open a tiny door and welcome the visitor.
Scroll → Vision & Mission.
Scroll → Short Kalapriti brand story.
Scroll → Product highlights with attractive product images.
Scroll → Instagram follow CTA.
Scroll → Founder/contact details and final thank-you message.
1. OVERALL VISUAL DIRECTION
Combine handmade warmth with a clean, modern landing-page experience.
Element
Direction

1. SECTION 01 — THE WELCOME / MAGIC DOOR
Objective
Create an immediate emotional connection and make the visitor curious enough to scroll.
Visual scene
A small cute illustrated/3D-looking door is placed in the centre.
Yarn balls, crochet hooks, flowers, tiny crochet accessories and/or yarn characters surround the door.
The yarn balls/accessories have cute eyes, tiny hands and tiny legs.
Characters should feel handmade rather than overly realistic.
The Kalapriti name/logo should appear naturally within the scene.
A soft yarn/thread path can visually guide the visitor toward the door.
Animation sequence
Page loads with a calm handmade background.
Cute yarn characters enter/appear around the door.
Characters look toward the visitor.
The door opens slowly as if inviting the visitor inside.
Characters wave or welcome the visitor.
Kalapriti logo/name appears.
Welcome message fades/slides in.
A small animated “Scroll to explore” indicator appears.
Exact on-screen copy
WELCOME TO KALAPRITI 
“loops of love from hands to heart”
Optional small CTA: “Come inside ↓”
1. SECTION 02 — VISION & MISSION
As the visitor scrolls, the door scene transitions into the brand introduction. A yarn/thread line can visually connect the sections.
Vision
To make handmade art a part of everyday life by creating meaningful, beautiful and personalized products that connect people through creativity, craftsmanship and emotion.
Mission
To create thoughtfully handcrafted crochet and personalized gifting products with love, creativity and attention to detail, while celebrating the value of handmade work and creating opportunities for skilled artisans and women.
Design suggestion
Show Vision and Mission as two soft cards or illustrated sections. Small yarn/crochet icons can animate gently when they enter the viewport.
1. SECTION 03 — THE KALAPRITI STORY
Keep this section short enough to read on a phone but emotional enough to communicate the heart behind the brand.
Suggested brand story copy
Kalapriti was born from a simple love for handmade art — the belief that something made by hand carries a feeling that mass-produced products cannot replace.
What started with yarn, a crochet hook and countless little loops grew into a world of handmade dolls, flowers, idols, keychains, gifts and personalized creations.
Every Kalapriti creation is made patiently, thoughtfully and with love — because a handmade gift is not just a product; it becomes a memory.
From one special gift to bulk orders and celebrations, Kalapriti brings creativity from our hands to your heart.
Optional visual treatment
Show a yarn thread travelling through the story.
Use small illustrations of a crochet hook, yarn ball, flower and finished product.
A short timeline-style animation can show: Yarn → Hands → Craft → Emotion → Kalapriti.
Keep animation subtle so the story remains easy to read.

1. QR CODE / SCANNER REQUIREMENTS
The QR code will be placed physically at the exhibition stall.
QR code should open the landing page directly.
Landing page must be mobile-first and responsive.
Loading time should be kept low despite animations and product images.
Use compressed WebP/optimized images where possible.
Provide a clear fallback if animation does not load.
Make all buttons large enough for easy mobile tapping.
1. ANIMATION & INTERACTION NOTES FOR DESIGNER
Use scroll-triggered animations rather than constant movement everywhere.
Characters can blink, wave, bounce slightly or move their hands.
The opening door is the hero animation and should be the most memorable interaction.
Yarn/thread can act as a recurring transition element between sections.
Product cards should have subtle hover/tap movement.
Keep animations smooth and lightweight.
Avoid excessive flashing or overly fast motion.
Respect reduced-motion settings if supported.

1. DESIGNER'S CREATIVE FREEDOM
The structure and copy define the required user journey. The designer may creatively interpret illustrations, character poses, transitions, typography pairing, product layout and micro-interactions while keeping the overall Kalapriti personality warm, handmade, premium and playful.
Most important feeling
When someone scans the QR code, they should think: “Wow, this brand is cute, creative and different.” The page should make the visitor feel like they have entered Kalapriti’s little handmade world.
